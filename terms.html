Terms of Service and Refund Policy

Clear Terms, in Writing

What we do, what we never promise, and how fees, cancellations and refunds work.

Effective date: November 3, 2026

VAA provides paid, nonlegal case support and does not guarantee recovery or any other result. A written agreement defines the work, fees and cancellation terms before paid work begins.

Who We Are

These Terms describe use of victimadvocacy.com and the case-support services of Victim Advocacy Associates LLC. Our work includes records organization, client coordination, source-referenced chronologies, evidence indexes and factual summaries for review by counsel or other appropriate professionals.

What We Are Not

  • We do not provide counseling, therapy or mental health treatment. If you need emotional support, please reach out to a licensed mental health professional.
  • We are not a law firm and do not give legal advice. Nothing on this website or in our work is legal advice. Using this website, sending a message, or engaging VAA does not create an attorney-client relationship with VAA. For legal advice, consult a licensed attorney.
  • We do not engage in the unauthorized practice of law. We do not appear as counsel, tell a client what the law requires, or make legal decisions that belong to the retaining attorney.
  • We are not licensed private investigators, and we do not perform licensed private-investigator work. Where a matter needs a licensed private investigator, accountant, or other licensed professional, that work is performed by an independent licensed professional under that professional’s own engagement.
  • We do not guarantee any outcome. We cannot promise that money will be recovered, that a case will be filed or won, or that an attorney will accept a matter.
  • Our fees are never tied to the outcome of a case. We do not take a percentage of any recovery. Fees are for the agreed work, whether or not the matter ends as anyone hoped.
  • We do not pay or accept referral fees to or from attorneys or anyone else.

Be cautious of unsolicited offers promising to recover lost funds for an upfront fee. VAA charges for agreed case-support work, does not guarantee recovery and does not request gift cards, cryptocurrency or payments wired to an individual's account.

How Engagements Work

Before paid work begins, we provide a written engagement agreement defining the scope, deliverables, fees, payment arrangements and anticipated timeline. Work starts after the agreement is signed and any stated start conditions are met. When a law firm retains VAA, the work is performed under the retaining attorney’s direction. The signed agreement controls if it conflicts with these website Terms, subject to applicable law.

Fees and Payment

  • One-matter pilot: $1,500 for up to 10 hours on a single matter. If the firm moves to the monthly partnership, that amount is credited toward the first month.
  • Monthly partnership: $2,500 a month, including up to 20 hours. Additional hours are $150 an hour. The arrangement is month to month and ends on 30 days written notice.
  • Hours included in a month are for that month. Unused hours do not roll over.
  • Records assessment: a fixed fee agreed in writing for an intake conversation, review of a defined set of materials and a written factual memo.
  • Case file development: a written project quote based on the agreed scope, record volume and deliverables, with any stages and payment schedule specified in the agreement.
  • Attorney support: project, hourly or ongoing arrangements as defined in the signed agreement with the engaging firm.
  • Victim impact statement: a flat fee agreed in writing for the interview, drafting support, revisions and coordination with the prosecutor’s office.

We will not expand the scope or add charges without written agreement. Your engagement agreement identifies the fees, any approved expenses and payment terms. Available payment methods are confirmed in advance, and payment instructions are included with your invoice.

Cancellations and Refunds

The refund terms below apply to fees paid before delivery.

  • Before work begins: you may cancel for a full refund of the fees paid.
  • Records assessment: if you cancel after work begins but before the written memo is delivered, the refund is reduced by work already performed, calculated at the rate stated in your agreement. Delivery of the agreed memo completes the assessment, subject to applicable rights.
  • Case file development: you may cancel at any time. Charges and any refund are based on the completed stages specified in your agreement. Prepaid amounts for unperformed work are refunded.
  • Ongoing support: cancel with 30 days written notice. Charges are based on work performed through the end of that notice, at the rate stated in your agreement, and prepaid amounts for work not performed are refunded.
  • Victim impact statement: if you cancel after work begins but before the final statement is delivered, the refund is reduced by work already performed, at the rate stated in your agreement. Delivery of the statement you approve completes the service.
  • If VAA cannot accept or continue the engagement, we will notify you and refund prepaid amounts for work not performed.

Request cancellation in writing at arpey@victimadvocacy.com. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 14 days of cancellation.

Work Engaged by Attorneys

When your firm engages VAA, we work under the agreed scope and the retaining attorney’s direction, and deliver work to the engaging attorney. The agreement identifies the responsible payer, authorized client contact, information-sharing permissions and any required client consent. Counsel determines whether and how VAA’s charges may be passed through to a client. Legal questions, strategy, and filings stay with the firm.

Confidentiality and Work Product

When a firm retains VAA, we treat the firm’s and the client’s nonpublic information as confidential and use it only for the agreed work. We follow the firm’s confidentiality instructions and will sign the firm’s nondisclosure agreement.

Chronologies, indexes, summaries, notes, and other materials prepared at counsel’s direction are prepared for the engaging attorney. We label them as the firm directs. Whether attorney-client privilege or work-product protection applies is the retaining attorney’s decision. Engaging VAA does not by itself create that protection.

We agree on how records are shared before any are sent. Do not send case files through this website. At the end of an engagement we return or destroy records as the firm directs and confirm that in writing.

If the law requires a report of suspected abuse or exploitation of an older or disabled adult, we make that report and tell the engaging firm promptly, where the law allows.

Limitation of Liability

To the fullest extent the law allows, VAA’s total liability arising out of an engagement is limited to the fees the retaining firm paid VAA for the services that gave rise to the claim. VAA is not liable for the firm’s legal advice, filings, strategy, or the outcome of a matter.

A signed engagement agreement may state a different cap. Nothing on this website limits liability for gross negligence, willful misconduct, or any liability the law does not allow a business to limit. Some states restrict limits of this kind. Where that is so, this limit applies only as far as the law allows.

The website is general information. We do not promise that every page is complete or current.

Indemnification

The retaining firm agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Victim Advocacy Associates LLC, and the people who work for it, from claims, losses, and reasonable expenses brought by the firm’s client or by a third party that arise out of the firm’s legal advice, case strategy, or instructions to VAA. This indemnity does not cover a claim to the extent it is caused by VAA’s gross negligence or willful misconduct.

The signed engagement agreement states the indemnity that applies to that engagement. If the agreement is silent, this section applies.
