If You Need an Attorney

Ask your own lawyer to engage us. If you don’t have a lawyer, your state bar’s lawyer referral service can help (in Texas, the State Bar of Texas, 800-252-9690), and you may qualify for free legal aid. You don’t need to tell us your immigration status to make that first call. The inquiry form doesn’t ask for it.

Our founder also works as a paid independent consultant for Bealke Law. Bealke Law pays nothing for referrals, and we will always tell you about this connection.

If You Are 65 or Older

Over 65? You’re not in trouble. If the law ever required us to report, the report would be about what someone did to you. Adult Protective Services may contact you to offer help. In most cases, you decide what help to accept. We explain this before you share details.

If You Are in Danger

These lines are free.

  • 911 If you are in immediate danger.
  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, by call or text, at any hour.
  • 800-799-7233 National Domestic Violence Hotline, at any hour. Or text START to 88788, or chat at thehotline.org ↗
  • 800-656-4673 RAINN National Sexual Assault Hotline, at any hour.
  • 844-762-8483 StrongHearts Native Helpline, for American Indians and Alaska Natives affected by domestic, dating or sexual violence. Call, text or chat at strongheartshelpline.org ↗
  • 866-331-9474 love is respect, for teens and young adults, at any hour. Or text LOVEIS to 22522.
  • Text HOME to 741741 Crisis Text Line, at any hour.
  • 800-252-5400 Texas Abuse Hotline, any hour, for suspected abuse of an adult 65 or older or an adult with a disability.
  • 833-372-8311 National Elder Fraud Hotline, if you are 60 or older.
  • 855-484-2846 VictimConnect, help finding victim services near you.

Victim Advocacy Associates is a private business, not a government or nonprofit victim services program. VAA does not provide legal representation or legal advice. We are not licensed private investigators and do not perform work that requires that license. If a matter needs it, a licensed professional does that work under their own engagement. We do not provide counseling, therapy or mental health treatment. Using this website or sending a message does not create an engagement or an attorney-client relationship with VAA. When a firm retains VAA, the work is performed under that attorney’s direction.
