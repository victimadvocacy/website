Accessibility

Help Using Our Website

We want everyone to be able to find information about VAA and reach us. If something on this site gets in your way, tell us and we’ll help another way.

Our Commitment

We want people of different abilities to be able to find information about VAA and contact us. We welcome feedback about barriers and requests for information in another format.

The Standard We Work Toward

Our accessibility goal is WCAG 2.2 Level AA. This is a development target, not a claim that the site has been audited or meets every requirement.

What We Are Working Toward

  • Keyboard access to navigation, links and forms, with visible focus and a way to skip repeated navigation.
  • Readable text, sufficient color contrast and layouts that remain usable when text is enlarged.
  • Useful text alternatives for meaningful images and appropriate handling of decorative images.
  • Controls to skip nonessential animation and support for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
  • Clearly labeled form fields and understandable error and confirmation messages, including for screen reader users.
  • No automatically playing audio.
  • Usable layouts on phones, tablets and computers.

Known Limitations

Some linked or third-party content may have accessibility limitations outside our control. Tell us about a barrier so we can review it and discuss another way to provide the information.

Need Help, or Found a Barrier?

If any part of this site is difficult to use, contact us and describe the help you need. We will discuss available alternatives, such as information by email or by phone at 817-805-2467.

Email arpey@victimadvocacy.com or use the contact page if it is accessible to you. Include the page, what went wrong and your preferred way to receive a response. Device or assistive-technology details are optional.
