Partnership Options

Start with a one-matter pilot. A monthly partnership then covers advocacy and litigation support across your matters, without hiring permanent staff.

  • One-Matter Pilot. $1,500 for up to 10 hours on a single matter. Credit toward your first month if you move to a monthly partnership.
  • Ongoing Monthly Partnership. Advocacy and litigation support across your matters, without hiring permanent staff. $2,500 a month, including up to 20 hours. Additional hours are $150 an hour. Unused hours do not roll over. Month to month. Cancel with 30 days written notice. The arrangement is set in writing.
  • After the Case. A defined transition with advocacy, resource coordination and follow-up. Length and fees are set in writing.
  • Client Referrals. Send a client through your firm. Advocacy stays under your direction.
  • One Matter. Advocacy and litigation support for a single engagement, when that is the right fit. Scope and fees are set in writing.
