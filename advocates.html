Independent Professionals We May Introduce

Investigative and Legal
Expertise When Needed

Beyond the people named here, VAA knows attorneys, licensed professional counselors (LPCs) and other mental health professionals, and practical services such as investigators, accountants and expert witnesses. We suggest only people we know, but we don’t judge legal or clinical skill, so ask anyone about their experience with matters like yours.

These professionals provide their services independently, under their own agreements, and are separately scoped. VAA provides advocacy and practical support; legal representation, licensed investigative work and therapy are provided by the appropriate professionals. See pricing.
