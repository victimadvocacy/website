Our Advocate Standard

Every Advocate Must Clear This Bar

Your lawyers stay on the legal work. Before an advocate works a firm’s matter, that advocate must complete every item below. This is VAA’s required process. It is not a certification that has been granted.

  • Trained to the NACP Standard. Every advocate must be trained to the NACP standard, aligned with NOVA’s National Advocate Credentialing Program, including at least 40 hours of victim advocacy training. This is not a claim that anyone holds NACP.
  • Criminal Background Check. Every advocate must complete a criminal background check before working a firm’s matter.
  • Confidentiality and Conflict Checks. Every advocate must sign confidentiality and conflict-check agreements before the file is opened. We also follow the firm’s confidentiality rules, agree how files are shared before anything is sent, and return or destroy them when the firm asks.
  • Trauma-Informed and Court-Accompaniment Training. Every advocate must complete trauma-informed training and court-accompaniment training.
  • Supervised Work Before Independent Work. Every advocate must complete supervised work on files before working a firm’s matter independently.
  • Ongoing Continuing Education. Every advocate must continue that education after beginning independent work.

Your firm keeps the legal work. We do not replace legal representation, and we do not provide counseling or therapy.
