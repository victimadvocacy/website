Our Approach

How We Work

Independent Professionals We Work With

When a matter calls for licensed work, VAA can introduce independent professionals we know well.

  • Each works independently, under their own engagement and license.
  • VAA does not share fees with them or direct their work.
  • Professional services are scoped separately.

How We Work with Clients

Our founder’s background in mental health peer support shapes how we approach sensitive conversations and client coordination.

  • Clients are the experts in their own lives.
  • Recovery after harm is rarely a straight line, so support is collaborative and paced to the person.
  • We follow your firm’s communication preferences and keep legal questions with your team.

Clear Commitments and an Honest Factual Record

We listen carefully and distinguish reported accounts from what the records establish.

  • Missing information and conflicting evidence stay visible.
  • Deliverables, timing and communication are defined in writing.
  • Changes are discussed before proceeding.

Our work is nonclinical advocacy, not counseling, therapy or mental health treatment. VAA’s advocacy complements the client’s legal team and does not replace legal representation or clinical care.

Litigation Support

Work Your Team Can Check

Every fact points to a source, so your team can verify the work before relying on it.

  • Each entry names its documentEvery line in a chronology or evidence index cites the record it came from, so your team can go straight to the source.
  • Reported amounts stay apart from confirmed onesWhat the client reports and what the records document are labeled separately and never blended into one figure.
  • Gaps stay visibleUnresolved dates, missing records and conflicting accounts are flagged for counsel, not filled in.
  • Drafted for counsel’s reviewChronologies, evidence indexes, loss summaries and factual memos come to your attorneys to check, edit or set aside.
  • Scope set in writingDeliverables, timing and communication are defined in writing, and changes are discussed before work proceeds.
  • Client follow-up stays with us. Legal questions stay with you.We handle routine calls, check-ins and records gathering. Anything legal is flagged and passed to counsel.

The Fine Print

The Details, When You Need Them

Our Services

We organize records, build source-referenced chronologies and evidence indexes, and summarize documented transactions for counsel’s review.

We help your client organize what happened, prepare for meetings with your team, and keep track of records and next steps. We coordinate with your firm under the engagement.

VAA does not perform licensed private investigation. Tasks requiring a license are handled by appropriately licensed professionals under a separate, agreed scope. We do not provide legal representation or legal advice.

What You Receive

  • Chronology. Relevant events from the reviewed materials, with source references and unresolved dates identified.
  • Evidence index. Records within the agreed scope, catalogued and labeled so counsel can locate them.
  • Documented-loss summary. Amounts and transactions supported by the records, with estimates and gaps clearly identified.
  • Factual memo. A concise account of what the reviewed records show, what is reported and what remains unresolved.

Scope, Fees & Professional Boundaries

  • No guaranteed outcome. We cannot promise recovery, attorney acceptance or a particular result.
  • No legal advice or representation. VAA organizes information for review; legal decisions belong to a licensed attorney.
  • No unlicensed professional services. Work requiring a license is handled by appropriately licensed professionals.
  • Written scope. Any additional work requires written agreement before proceeding. See pricing.
