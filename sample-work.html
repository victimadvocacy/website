The Matter, in Brief

Client A, age 81, lives alone. Her daughter noticed money leaving her checking account after a paid caregiver, Caregiver B, was added to it. Counsel asked VAA to organize the records, separate what is confirmed from what is reported, and identify what is still missing.

What’s Still Missing

Gaps are named so counsel can decide what to request next. Whether and how to obtain each record is counsel’s decision.

  • Ownership records for the account ending 7781.
  • Branch records or video for the 05/10/2025 cash withdrawal.
  • Any written caregiving or pay agreement between Client A and Caregiver B.
  • Photos, receipts or an appraisal for the missing jewelry.
