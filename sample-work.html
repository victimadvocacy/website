Deliverable 4

What’s Still Missing

Gaps are named so counsel can decide what to request next. Whether and how to obtain each record is counsel’s decision.

  • Ownership records for the account ending 7781.
  • Branch records or video for the 05/10/2025 cash withdrawal.
  • Any written caregiving or pay agreement between Client A and Caregiver B.
  • Photos, receipts or an appraisal for the missing jewelry.
  • Login and device records for the online transfers.
  • Any fraud claim Client A or her daughter filed with the bank, and the bank’s response.
  • Statements after July 2025.
  • Any record of Caregiver B’s pay rate or hours worked.
  • The case number for the family’s report to Adult Protective Services.

VAA organizes records for review by counsel and does not provide legal advice or perform licensed investigation.
