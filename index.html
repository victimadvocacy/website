Matters We Support

Matters Your Firm Can Bring

See a Sample File

Whatever happened, the work is the same: organize the record, keep the person’s own account intact and keep counsel informed. Here is what that looks like in different matters.

Financial Fraud and Scams

Payment records, messages and a timeline organized so counsel can see what happened and what still needs investigation.

Domestic Abuse and Coercive Control

Patient help organizing communications and documenting reported incidents, so a history that’s hard to put into words is clear for counsel.

Sexual Assault and Abuse

Sensitive conversations at the client’s pace, and materials for counsel that keep the client’s own account intact.

Stalking and Harassment

Incidents and messages organized into a chronology that shows the pattern, with the records still needed named.

Elder Abuse and Financial Exploitation

Financial records organized clearly, including misuse of a power of attorney, trust or guardianship, with the older client involved in explaining what happened.

Workplace Harassment, Discrimination and Retaliation

Workplace messages and employment records put in order, with a clear timeline and missing information flagged.

Serious Injury and Negligence

Help keeping up with records requests and paperwork during recovery, and a record of how the injury affects daily life.

Identity Theft and Account Misuse

Disputed transactions, account notices and letters organized to show what’s been reported, what came back and what’s still open.

Violent Crime Victims and Their Families

Records requests tracked, information organized and questions prepared for counsel, so the family has someone keeping track of the details.

Institutional Misconduct and Civil Rights

Incident records and complaint letters organized, with the client’s reports kept separate from what the documents establish.
