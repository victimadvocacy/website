Why we exist

They have carried enough.

Someone who has been wronged hires an attorney to stand for them in court. Yet the work of gathering the proof has always fallen back on their own shoulders, at the very moment they have the least left to give.

We believe they have carried enough. Attorneys belong in the courtroom, not buried in bank statements. Between them stands Victim Advocacy Associates, turning what happened into what holds up.

Two ways to bring us in.

About us

Built by someone who has stood where you are standing.

Victim Advocacy Associates began with Ally Arpey's own experience of fraud. She knows the disbelief of the first discovery, the long nights spent rebuilding what happened from statements and messages, and how alone it feels to carry the proof while still carrying the loss.

She came through it determined that no one else should have to face that work by themselves. Today she brings the same patience and care, shaped by her training in clinical counseling, to every person who comes through the door, and the same rigor to every file that goes on to an attorney.

Victim Advocacy Associates does not provide counseling, therapy or mental health treatment.

That is the heart of this practice. We listen as people who understand, and we build as people who know what is at stake.

Why we do this

The truth leaves a trail.

Fraud depends on silence, on people feeling too ashamed, too tired, or too alone to piece together what happened. But every transfer leaves a trace, and every message carries a date. What drives us is the moment the people who were harmed can finally rest, knowing the truth was heard.
