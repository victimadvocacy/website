The Records

Every Record in One Source-Cited Timeline

We turn the records your firm provides into a dated chronology and loss summary, with every entry tied to its source.

The Paper Trail

Your Client’s Evidence, Gathered and Organized

We help your client find and export their own texts, emails, and statements, then organize them into a file your team can use.

The Numbers

Documented Losses, Kept Apart from Reported Ones

We check each amount against bank and platform records and keep reported losses separate from documented ones.

The Filings

Court Filings Indexed for Fast Review

Under your direction, we organize the court filings your firm provides into an index your team can check, with the source cited for each entry.

The Courtroom

A Client Prepared for Every Hearing

We help your client draft and edit their victim impact statement in their own words, and help character witnesses write letters that sound like them, not a template. As your attorney directs, we prepare your client for what to expect and go to court with them.

The Client

Fewer Routine Client Calls for Your Attorneys

We handle routine calls, check-ins and updates between attorney conversations, keep your client supported and on track, and pass every legal question back to your team.
