Our Advocate Standard

Every Advocate Must Clear This Bar

Your lawyers stay on the legal work. Before an advocate works a firm’s matter, that advocate must complete every item below. This is VAA’s required process. It is not a certification that has been granted.

  • Trained to the NACP Standard. Advocates are trained to the NACP standard, aligned with NOVA’s National Advocate Credentialing Program, including at least 40 hours of victim advocacy training. This is not a claim that anyone holds NACP.
  • Criminal Background Check. Required before the advocate works a firm’s matter.
  • Signed Agreements. Confidentiality and conflict-check agreements.
  • Trauma-Informed and Court Training. Trauma-informed training and court-accompaniment training.
  • Supervised Files. Supervised work on files before independent work.
  • Continuing Education. Ongoing continuing education after that.
