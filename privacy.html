Privacy Policy

How We Handle Your Information

This policy explains the information we collect, why we use it and the choices available to you.

Effective date: October 24, 2026

Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how Victim Advocacy Associates LLC (“Victim Advocacy Associates,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses and protects personal information through victimadvocacy.com and in the course of our work.

We use personal information for the purposes described in this policy. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

What We Collect

Information You Give Us

  • Contact form: your name, email address, your state, phone number, preferred language and safe times to call if you give them, whether you are an attorney, individual or family member, your firm’s name if you give it, the type of support you need, and the message you write.
  • When you engage us: the information needed to provide our services, which may include financial statements, account records, correspondence, and other documents about the matter. See Case records you entrust to us.
  • Payments: our payment processor handles payment details. VAA receives the information needed for billing and transaction records.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Technical information: our website and service providers may receive information such as your IP address, browser type and pages requested when you visit.
  • Fonts and other embedded services: where a page loads content from an external provider, that provider may receive technical information, such as your IP address. The services we use are named under Cookies, Tracking and Do Not Track below.

How We Use It

  • To respond to your inquiry and explain our services and fees
  • To provide the services you engage us for, under a written agreement
  • To coordinate authorized work with your attorney or other professionals involved in the matter
  • To bill for our services and keep business records
  • To keep the website secure and working
  • To meet legal obligations

We use inquiry details to respond to your request. Any separate marketing use will be explained and handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

Who We Share It With

We share personal information only as described here:

  • Service providers that support website hosting, inquiry delivery, email, record storage or transfer, and payments, to the extent needed for their agreed services.
  • Your attorney and other professionals involved in your matter, as authorized and permitted.
  • When required by law, such as in response to a valid subpoena or court order, or to protect someone’s safety.
  • If our business changes hands, information may transfer to a successor that agrees to protect it as this policy describes.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not disclose it to third parties for their own direct marketing.

Case Records You Entrust to Us

We use case records to perform the agreed work and manage the engagement. Access, authorized sharing, retention and return or deletion are governed by the engagement agreement and applicable obligations.

Please do not send sensitive records through the contact form or ordinary email. Contact us first for the agreed method of sharing materials.

VAA is not a law firm. Communications with us are not automatically protected by attorney-client privilege. Ask your attorney how privilege or work-product rules may apply before sharing sensitive information.

Cookies, Tracking and Do Not Track

Cookies and analytics. Our website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and it does not track you across other sites.

Embedded services. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives technical information such as your IP address when a page loads. Our website host and contact-form provider also process technical information needed to deliver the site and your inquiry.

Browser privacy signals. Because we don’t sell personal information, share it for targeted advertising or track visitors across sites, signals such as Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track don’t change how our website treats your information. If that changes, we will update this policy and honor Global Privacy Control where the law requires.

Security and Retention

We use safeguards appropriate to the information we handle. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We will provide breach notices when required by applicable law.

We retain inquiries and engagement records for the purposes and periods described in our retention practices and written agreements, subject to applicable legal and preservation obligations.

Your Choices and Rights

You may ask us at any time to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you
  • correct information that is inaccurate
  • request deletion, subject to applicable exceptions and retention obligations
  • stop contacting you

Send privacy requests to arpey@victimadvocacy.com. We may need to verify your identity or authority to make a request. We will explain any applicable limits and respond within the time required by law. We do not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

Children

The website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 through it. If you believe a child has submitted information, contact us so we can review and address it.

Changes and Contact

We will post updates here with the effective date. We will provide additional notice when required by law or an applicable agreement.
