Your Firm Leads the Case. We Support Your Client and Your Team.

A dedicated advocate stays with your client through hearings and follow-up calls. Organized, source-tied records come back to your team. The work stays under your firm’s direction, and legal questions stay with counsel. Nothing is binding until you sign a written agreement.

What the Firm Receives

  • Source-referenced chronologies, evidence indexes and loss summaries your team can check line by line.
  • Reported amounts kept apart from confirmed ones.
  • A point of contact for the client’s calls, appointments and paperwork. Legal questions still come to you.
  • Victim impact statement support in the client’s own words, and accompaniment at hearings.
  • Work with firms in every state, by phone and video, and travel for hearings when a matter needs it.

Fees Already in Writing

  • Free 15-minute call.
  • One-Matter Pilot: $1,500 up to 10 hours on a single matter. Credit toward your first month if you move to a monthly partnership.
  • $2,500/month ongoing client and case support, including up to 20 hours. Additional hours are $150 an hour. Unused hours do not roll over. Month to month. Cancel with 30 days written notice.
  • $1,500–$6,000 typical for comprehensive case work. Larger matters are quoted in stages.
  • $500 fixed-fee records assessment.
  • $450 flat fee for a victim impact statement.
  • $150/hour for work outside a defined project. The same rate applies to additional hours on the monthly plan.

Every engagement starts with a written proposal. Scope, price and payment terms are agreed in writing before work begins. Fees are never contingent on a recovery, and we never pay or accept referral fees.

How an Engagement Starts

  • Before work starts, we confirm the scope, the records needed, the deliverables and the delivery schedule.
  • The target for a records assessment is five business days after you accept the scope and we receive the agreed records.
  • We return calls and messages within one business day, and answer work requests during an engagement within two business days.
  • We agree how records are shared before anything is sent, and return or destroy them when the firm asks.

Insurance

Commercial general liability through Hiscox: $1 million per occurrence, $2 million aggregate. Certificate on request.

Describing VAA to Your Clients

Firms may use this description only after engaging VAA in writing:

Our firm works with an independent victim advocacy company that can offer clients additional practical support during the legal process and, when arranged, after their case concludes. It is not a law firm and does not give legal advice. This allows our legal team to remain focused on representation while clients receive dedicated nonlegal assistance.

Victim Advocacy Associates is a private business, not a government or nonprofit victim services program. It is not a law firm and does not provide legal representation or legal advice. We are not licensed private investigators; work that requires a license is performed by licensed professionals. We do not provide counseling, therapy or mental health treatment.

Our founder also works as an independent consultant for Bealke Law; if we ever suggest that firm, we’ll tell you, and it pays us nothing for introductions.
