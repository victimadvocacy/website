VAA provides paid, nonlegal advocacy and litigation support within the box above. That support can include client coordination, help gathering records the client is entitled to provide, source-referenced chronologies, evidence indexes, factual summaries, hearing accompaniment, and related practical support. VAA does only the work this agreement describes.

VAA does not provide legal representation, legal advice, counseling, therapy, or mental health treatment.

## 3. Direction by Counsel

The responsible attorney directs the work. VAA follows that attorney's instructions on scope, client contact, and what may be shared.

Clients contact the firm for legal advice, case updates, and strategy. VAA does not interpret the firm's advice, speak for the firm, or make commitments for the firm.

VAA copies the firm on emails about the matter, gives the firm a summary of calls, and does not send material to the client unless the firm knows.

## 4. No Legal Advice and No Attorney-Client Relationship with VAA

VAA is not a law firm. Nothing VAA says or writes is legal advice. This agreement does not create an attorney-client relationship between VAA and the firm, or between VAA and any client of the firm.

Communications with VAA are not automatically protected by attorney-client privilege. The firm decides what protection, if any, applies.

## 5. No Guarantee, No Contingent Fee, No Referral Fee

VAA does not guarantee any outcome, recovery, filing, or result.

Fees are for the agreed work. They are not contingent on the outcome of a case, and VAA does not take a percentage of any recovery.

VAA does not pay or accept referral fees.

## 6. No Unauthorized Practice of Law and No Licensed Private-Investigator Work

VAA does not practice law. VAA does not appear as counsel, draft legal arguments as the attorney, or decide what the law requires.

VAA is not a licensed private investigator and does not perform work that requires that license. When licensed investigative, legal, clinical, or accounting work is needed, it is performed by an independent licensed professional under that professional's own engagement. VAA does not direct that work and does not share fees for it.

## 7. Confidentiality

VAA will keep confidential the nonpublic information of the firm and of the client, and will use it only to perform this agreement. VAA will follow the firm's confidentiality instructions.

If the firm asks, VAA will sign the firm's nondisclosure agreement. If that agreement conflicts with this section, the firm's nondisclosure agreement controls on confidentiality, except where this agreement states a legal duty that cannot be waived.

VAA runs a conflicts check before opening a matter and will not work for a person on the other side of the same matter or a closely related matter.

If the law requires a report of suspected abuse or exploitation of an older or disabled adult, VAA will make that report and will tell the firm promptly where the law allows.

VAA does not record client conversations unless the firm asks in writing and the client agrees. If a recording is made, VAA follows the firm's instructions on storage and on who receives it.

## 8. Work Product

Materials prepared at counsel's direction, including chronologies, indexes, summaries, and notes, are prepared for the engaging attorney. The firm decides how those materials are labeled. Whether work-product protection or privilege applies is the firm's decision. Engaging VAA does not by itself create that protection.

The firm and VAA will agree on how records are shared before any are sent. Ordinary email and the website contact form are not the method for case files.

When the engagement ends, VAA will return or destroy records as the firm directs and will confirm that in writing.

## 10. Termination

Either party may end the monthly partnership with 30 days written notice to the other. VAA charges for work performed through the end of that notice. Amounts paid for work not performed are refunded.

Before work begins, the firm may cancel and receive a full refund of fees paid.

Either party may end this agreement sooner if the other party does not cure a material breach within a reasonable time after written notice, or if a legal or professional duty requires the work to stop.

Notice under this agreement is written notice by email to the addresses above, or to any later address a party gives in writing.

> To the fullest extent the law allows, VAA's total liability arising out of this agreement is limited to the fees the firm actually paid VAA for the services that gave rise to the claim. VAA is not liable for the firm's legal advice, filings, strategy, or the outcome of a matter.

## 12. Indemnity

Counsel should rewrite this section before use.

Suggested formulation:

> The firm will indemnify and hold harmless VAA, and the people who work for it, from claims, losses, and reasonable expenses brought by the firm's client or by a third party that arise out of the firm's legal advice, case strategy, or instructions to VAA, except to the extent caused by VAA's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

## 13. Insurance Note for Counsel

Commercial general liability through Hiscox: $1 million per occurrence, $2 million aggregate. Certificate on request.

This draft does not state that VAA carries professional liability insurance or errors and omissions insurance.

## 14. Governing Law and Disputes

Texas law governs this agreement, without regard to conflict-of-law rules.

Counsel should confirm the venue before use. Suggested venue, because VAA's mailing address is in Colleyville: the state or federal courts located in Tarrant County, Texas.

The parties will try to resolve a dispute by writing to each other first.

## 15. Entire Agreement

This signed agreement is the entire agreement for the services it covers. It replaces earlier conversations about that scope. It can be changed only by a writing signed by both parties, except that a written email from both sides can change scope or fees if both sides clearly agree.
