How Does VAA Work with Our Team?

Start with a defined project, a pilot matter or ongoing support. We agree on services, fees, authorized client contact and a reporting plan in writing. VAA follows your communication preferences; clients continue to contact your team directly for legal advice, case updates and strategy. We work with firms in every state, by phone and video, and travel for hearings when a matter needs it.

Could VAA Be Called as a Witness?

Possibly. Anyone who gathers or organizes records in a matter can be asked to testify or produce materials. We prepare our work so it can stand up to that: every entry is tied to its source, and our methods are documented. Engaging VAA at counsel’s direction, and labeling work product accordingly, gives your team the strongest position to evaluate protection. Whether that protection applies is counsel’s call. If we receive a subpoena or other request about a matter, we notify the engaging firm promptly, where the law permits.

How Do You Protect Client Records?

We agree on a secure way to exchange records before any are sent, and we never ask for sensitive records by ordinary email or through our contact form. Access is limited to the people working on the matter. If your firm has its own data-handling requirements, we follow them. At the end of an engagement we return or destroy records as your firm directs and confirm in writing.

Do You Follow Our Firm’s Confidentiality Rules?

Yes, with one exception. We follow your firm’s confidentiality and supervision instructions and will sign your nondisclosure agreement. If the law requires us to report suspected abuse or exploitation of an older or disabled adult, we will, and we’ll tell your firm first where the law allows. We run a conflicts check before taking any matter and won’t work on a matter adverse to a firm’s client in a related dispute.

What Experience Does VAA Bring?

VAA works directly with litigation teams on behalf of people harmed by exploitation, abuse and breach of trust, building source-referenced chronologies, evidence indexes and documented-loss summaries and coordinating with clients throughout. Our approach is informed by our founder’s background in mental health peer mentoring, case management and crisis management, and we support clients in workplace-proficient Spanish. When a matter needs licensed work, we introduce independent professionals, including a trial lawyer and a licensed private investigator. Recent matters are described on our work page.

How Do You Keep Our Team Informed?

Before work begins, we agree in writing on what we report, to whom and how often. By default we provide short written updates covering practical support, upcoming dates and anything that should go to your team. We don’t record client conversations unless your firm directs it in writing, because a verbatim record of a client’s account can raise privilege and discovery questions that counsel should decide. When a firm does request recordings, we record only with the client’s consent and follow the firm’s instructions on storage, transcription and access.

Can Support Continue After the Case Ends?

Yes, within an agreed transition plan and duration. Support may include following up on referrals, coordinating resources and helping the client take practical next steps.

Can You Help Us Find an Expert Witness?

Yes. We can introduce your team to expert witnesses from our professional network, all vetted by VAA before we refer them. Experts are retained by and work directly with counsel under their own terms, and VAA does not pay or accept referral fees.

How Are Fees and Professional Roles Handled?

VAA provides paid, nonlegal services. Scope, fees, payer, permissions and duration are agreed before work begins. Fees are not contingent on a recovery, and VAA does not pay or accept referral fees. Licensed legal, clinical and investigative work belongs to appropriately licensed professionals.

VAA's involvement does not itself establish attorney-client privilege or work-product protection; counsel evaluates those issues for the matter.
