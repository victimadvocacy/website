Read Ally’s Full Biography

Ally helps attorneys build a clearer factual foundation for matters involving fraud, financial abuse, fiduciary misconduct and other harm. As founder of Victim Advocacy Associates, she takes on the detailed work of reconstructing events, organizing financial records, developing evidence chronologies and coordinating client communication. Her work gives counsel organized material to assess, focused questions to pursue and a more coherent understanding of the matter. She founded VAA in 2026 to build this work into a business, alongside her continuing work as an independent consultant under a services agreement effective April 2026.

Her value lies in connecting information across records and client accounts. She examines how dates, transactions and communications fit together, identifies inconsistencies for counsel’s review and helps clients tell their story clearly, in their own words. This gives attorneys a practical resource for working through fragmented information while keeping legal evaluation and strategy with counsel.

Ally’s background directly supports this work. She earned a B.A. in Organizational Communication, with a minor in Marketing, from Pepperdine University, grounding her approach in how information is gathered, structured and communicated. She is pursuing a Master of Arts in Clinical Mental Health Counseling at Chadron State College, in a CACREP-accredited program, with a 4.0 GPA. She is expected to graduate in December 2026, completing graduate training toward licensure as a Licensed Professional Counselor. She also speaks Spanish at a workplace-proficient level.

Ally came to this work after seeing fraud up close. What stayed with her was how alone the people it touches are: nobody talks about fraud, and the shame keeps people quiet. Most don’t know what to do first or who to call. VAA exists so the next person does.

Ally is a counseling student, not a licensed counselor. VAA’s support is nonclinical advocacy: Victim Advocacy Associates does not provide legal advice, legal representation, counseling, therapy or mental health treatment.
