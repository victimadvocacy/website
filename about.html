About Us

Steady Support for Clients. Clear Records for Counsel.

Victim Advocacy Associates gives law firms dedicated victim advocacy and litigation support, without the commitment of in-house overhead.

What We Do

A Dedicated Advocacy Partner for Your Firm

VAA works alongside law firms on matters involving fraud, financial abuse, elder and vulnerable-adult abuse, fiduciary misconduct, family violence and personal injury.

We help clients stay supported and on track through the legal process their firm manages, and we organize the records the firm provides into materials counsel can rely on. The result: attorneys stay focused on the law, and clients have a consistent person who listens, follows through and stays engaged.

Client Advocacy

Between attorney updates, we handle routine calls and check-ins, help clients prepare for meetings and hearings, and keep them supported and on track.

Litigation Support

We turn scattered records into source-referenced chronologies, evidence indexes and documented-loss summaries your team can check.

Under Your Direction

Your firm runs the case. Legal judgment and strategy stay with counsel, and every legal question goes back to your team.

Why We Exist

Our Mission and Goal

Our Mission

To help attorneys build a clearer factual record while giving people affected by fraud, financial abuse, and fiduciary misconduct steady, practical support. We organize complex information, listen carefully, and follow through so clients can participate in their cases without carrying every practical burden alone.

Our Goal

To make demanding cases more manageable for the attorneys leading them and the people living through them. We want legal teams to have organized, source-referenced materials they can assess, and clients to have a consistent person helping them gather records, prepare for meetings, and follow through on next steps. Our work stays under the firm’s direction, with legal advice and strategy remaining with counsel.

Meet Our Founder

Ally Arpey

Founder & Litigation Consultant

Ally Arpey founded Victim Advocacy Associates to give attorneys a dedicated partner for the detailed records work and sustained client support that complex cases demand. She works on matters involving fraud, financial abuse, and fiduciary misconduct, turning scattered documents and client accounts into organized records, source-referenced timelines, and focused questions for counsel’s review.

Her approach took shape while helping a woman uncover years of financial exploitation by someone she had trusted and supported. Ally helped piece together financial records, years of messages, and information from other people affected. She organized the documentation and helped coordinate information for law enforcement while staying alongside the woman as she confronted the betrayal, reported what had happened, and navigated an unfamiliar process.

That experience shaped the purpose of VAA. Ally saw how much can fall on someone who is already dealing with financial loss: locating records, reconstructing events, explaining a complicated history, and keeping up with requests for information. She built her work around helping carry that burden, giving attorneys usable materials and clients someone who listens, follows through, and stays engaged.

Today, she provides records organization and practical client support under the firm’s direction. Attorneys retain responsibility for legal judgment and strategy, while Ally helps clients gather information, prepare for meetings, and follow through on the firm’s requests.

VAA provides nonclinical advocacy and litigation support, not counseling, therapy, or mental health treatment. VAA is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice or legal representation.
