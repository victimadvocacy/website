Read Ally’s Full Biography

Ally Arpey founded Victim Advocacy Associates to give attorneys a dedicated partner for the detailed records work and sustained client support that complex cases demand. She works on matters involving fraud, financial abuse, and fiduciary misconduct, turning scattered documents and client accounts into organized records, source-referenced timelines, and focused questions for counsel’s review.

Her approach took shape while helping a woman uncover years of financial exploitation by someone she had trusted and supported. Ally helped piece together financial records, years of messages, and information from other people affected. She organized the documentation and helped coordinate information for law enforcement while staying alongside the woman as she confronted the betrayal, reported what had happened, and navigated an unfamiliar process.

That experience shaped the purpose of VAA. Ally saw how much can fall on someone who is already dealing with financial loss: locating records, reconstructing events, explaining a complicated history, and keeping up with requests for information. She built her work around helping carry that burden, giving attorneys usable materials and clients someone who listens, follows through, and stays engaged.

Today, she provides records organization and practical client support under the firm’s direction. Attorneys retain responsibility for legal judgment and strategy, while Ally helps clients gather information, prepare for meetings, and follow through on the firm’s requests.

Her graduate training in clinical mental health counseling informs how she listens and approaches sensitive conversations. Ally is a counseling student, not a licensed counselor. VAA provides nonclinical advocacy and litigation support, not counseling, therapy, or mental health treatment. VAA is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice or legal representation.
