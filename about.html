Meet Our Founder

Ally Arpey

Founder & Litigation Consultant

Beside your client. Behind your case.

Experience

  • Graduate training. Clinical mental health counseling at Chadron State College, graduating December 2026.
  • Counseling student. Ally is a counseling student, not a licensed counselor.
  • Case management and mental health settings. Peer mentoring, case management, and crisis management shape how she listens and stays with a client.
  • Law settings. Litigation support in law settings, including a long-running fraud matter. That work shaped how she organizes financial records and follows through with the client.

Ally Arpey helps attorneys turn fragmented records and complicated client histories into a clearer factual foundation. On fraud, elder abuse, fiduciary, trust and estate, and other matters where a client needs steady support, she organizes financial records, builds chronologies with sources, and flags gaps and inconsistencies for counsel’s review. She also stays with the client, helping gather information, prepare for meetings, and follow through on the firm’s requests.

Her work on a long-running fraud matter helped shape that approach. She saw how much work sits between experiencing fraud and being able to explain what happened: piecing together records, navigating unfamiliar processes, and finding someone willing to listen and follow through. VAA brings that attention to both the documentation and the person living through the case.

For attorneys, that means organized materials they can assess and dedicated support for the client communication and practical coordination a demanding matter requires. Ally works under the firm’s direction.

Her graduate training in clinical mental health counseling informs how she listens, approaches sensitive conversations, and helps clients communicate difficult experiences in their own words.
